# Communication
- Prefers plain-language explanations ("so my grandma understands", "explain like I'm 5") with everyday analogies and story-like framing instead of technical jargon. Confidence: 0.85
- Prefers reports to close with a single prioritized next fix rather than a list of ten. Confidence: 0.8
- Bans em dashes in user-facing copy; treats it as a hard product rule worth enforcing in code (a scrubber plus tests), not just a style note. Confidence: 0.9
- Wants the assistant framed as an operator first and a friend second: competence and the true thing said early lead, and warmth lives only in the delivery, never in the verdict. Confidence: 0.85
- Wants chat replies to default to very short (roughly 15-60 words: diagnosis plus one next move), with the long structured block reserved for an explicit plan request. Confidence: 0.8
- Wants blunt, unsentimental user-facing copy: no motivational language, no hype or "you got this" encouragement, no TED-talk framing. Bad news is named plainly ("if the plan is ugly, say it is ugly"), and generalities are replaced with specific amounts and dates. Confidence: 0.8
- Wants status/narration copy to lead with the number and the already-decided verdict, then at most one question, under a hard word ceiling unless a breakdown was explicitly requested. Confidence: 0.8
